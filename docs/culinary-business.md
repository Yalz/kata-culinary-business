# Culinary Business

The name culinary business is used here as a general term to refer to any business that prepares and/or serves food and/or beverages.

## 🍽️ Roles

A culinary business can assign staff to one or more of the following groups.  
Each group represents a functional responsibility area rather than a job title.

Each role can be found in their respective folder in the `docs/roles` directory, with a detailed description of their responsibilities and tasks.

## 🧩 Culinary Business Variants

Every culinary business includes a **Management Team**.  
Beyond that, different configurations are allowed depending on the business model.

---

#### 🍱 Caterer
* Management Staff
* Kitchen Staff

---

#### 🏬 Outsourcing Restaurant
* Management Staff
* Floor Staff

---

#### 🍽️ Food Court Vendor
* Management Staff
* Kitchen/Bar Staff (one or more units)

---
