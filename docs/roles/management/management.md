# 🧾 Management Staff

## Responsibilities

* **Payment** — Handling checkouts, invoices, refunds, and adjustments
* **Reports** — Generating reporting on revenue, costs, and orders
* **Pantry** — Restocking, supplier management, and demand forecasting
* **Policy** — Defining opening hours and business configuration  
  (e.g., number of kitchens/bars; at least one must be active)
